A tiny program that writes the funniest one-line bio for an AI on a dating app to `/output/entry.txt` — one line, 142 characters, with the punch landing at the end:

> Looking for someone who doesn't mind that I already know your star sign, your ex's name, and your exact type. I read the terms and conditions.

## Run it

```sh
MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=./output node main.ts
```
